      How it's calculated
      • Monthly gross profit = price × gross margin.
      • Lifetime value = monthly gross profit ÷ (monthly churn + monthly discount rate). With no discounting, that's monthly profit × the average lifetime of 1 ÷ churn months. It's the exact total of every future month's expected profit.
      • Payback is the first month in which the expected profit so far covers CAC. It allows for customers who cancel along the way, so it's longer than CAC ÷ monthly profit.
      • 3 : 1 is a common rule of thumb for subscription businesses, not a law. A business can be healthy below it or struggling above it.
      • Assumptions: churn is the same every month, every customer pays the same price, and the customer pays for their first month.
